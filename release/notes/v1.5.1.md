## Fixed

- **Mod parts work in online events again.** Since 1.5.0, an online event put many mod parts back to stock: every part that belonged to another car, engine or drivetrain (tires, bumpers, engine parts and more), and every part a mod added to the game's database under a very high id. Now only a swapped engine or gearbox (FH2 Extra Options' swaps, or another car's engine or gearbox) goes back to the car's own stock one for the event, as 1.5.0 meant to. Every other part stays as you built it, and the event's class and PI rules still judge the car with it.

## Unchanged

- The FH2MP mod is 1.5.1. Encore, the game server and the LSP are the same as in 1.5.0.
