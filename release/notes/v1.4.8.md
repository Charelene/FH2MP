## Updated

- **HonkHonk 1.0.0** replaces 0.4.0; the launcher installs it the next time you press Play or run setup. What it brings:
  - 47 horn sounds instead of 31: the Police2, Police3, Martinshorn and Firetruck sirens, plus Cat, Meow, Siren, Sleigh Bells, Wheelspin, Win10, Xbox, Nyooom, La Cucaracha, Cyberpunk, Rizz and Stinky.
  - On FH2 Mod Platform 2.9.1 every sound is in one Horn sound row (Pause > Settings > Mods); press A on it for the list.
  - The four new sirens sit right after Police, so a sound you picked from Boat onwards in 0.4.0 is now a different one. Pick it again in Pause > Settings > Mods.

## Changed

- **Other players hear every HonkHonk sound**, the new sirens too. Players still on an older FH2MP hear the 31 sounds of 0.4.0 as before and don't hear the new ones.
- **A held horn sounds for up to a minute** for the other players instead of 10 seconds, so long sirens play out. Players on an older FH2MP still stop it after 10 seconds.

## Unchanged

- The FH2MP mod (shim 1.4.8), the launcher (1.4.8) and the game server are new; the LSP is the same.
