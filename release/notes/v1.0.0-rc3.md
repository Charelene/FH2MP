> **Release candidate 3.** Please report bugs and feedback in [Issues](https://github.com/Charelene/FH2MP/issues).

## What's new in rc3

### Added
- Cars that can't be obtained in the game (traffic vehicles, hidden variants) are blocked online.

### Changed
- Speed trap leaderboards are separate per trap.
- Rival times can no longer beat the posted world records.
- King and Infection pick the first infected player or the King while the starting grid is up.

### Fixed
- The game closing for everyone in a lobby when a player with a custom design joined.
- "Finding session" never ending after a disconnect.
- Custom liveries not showing on other players' cars.
- King and Infection: wrong mode, "Setting Up Event" hangs, and the event not ending.
- "Leave Club" did nothing. A leader who leaves disbands the club.
- The Online Road Trip tile on the Home screen was locked.
- Dev-channel players ending up on the live game server.
