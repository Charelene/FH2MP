## Changed

- **FH2 Extra Options 0.6.0.** FH2MP now uses the newest Extra Options (by RokkuDayo), so you can keep it if you've already installed it. If you haven't, the launcher installs it the next time you press Play. Your settings in `FH2_ExtraOptions.ini` stay as they are, and your old `winmm.dll` is kept in the `fh2extra` folder.
- **Your mods move to `mods\Forza Horizon 2`.** Extra Options 0.6.0 keeps its mods there, and the first time the game starts with it, it moves the mods from your `mods` folder there itself. Car packs keep working online, as before.
- **Extra Options' own Garage Revving and Weather Control mods** come with 0.6.0 in `mods\Forza Horizon 2`. The older copies the launcher put in `mods` move to `fh2extra\mods-0.4.4`.
- This update is required: launchers older than 1.3.3 are asked to update before the next Play.

## Fixed

- **Weather Control's time lock online.** Weather Control's time lock is set aside while you're online again, so your clock matches everyone else's, and it comes back when you leave. This works with the Weather Control in Extra Options 0.6.0 too.
- **Extra Options loaded no mods at all** if you had tried 0.6.0 and an older launcher put 0.4.4 back: you were left with two mod lists. When every mod is already in `mods\Forza Horizon 2`, the launcher renames the old list. Otherwise it tells you which folders to move.
