## New

- **Showdowns at the Car Meet.** "Start Showdown Race" in the Car Meet menu works online again. Everyone at the meet can join or leave while the countdown runs. The grid is ordered by PI, lowest first, and the slower cars get a head start: the faster cars wait at the line and see how many seconds are left. Once the first car finishes, the race runs on for the game's own end-of-race countdown, so the others can still finish.
- **The group route in online Free Roam.** A group waypoint set on the map shows its route on everyone's map and GPS again. In a public Free Roam anyone can set it; in a private Free Roam only the leader can.

## Fixed

- **Players' cars climbing onto each other and shaking when they touch.** When you pushed or bumped another player's car, your game moved its copy of that car, so it ended up on your bonnet on your screen and shook on its driver's screen. Other players' cars now stay where their drivers have them, and a push between two players is solid on both screens.
- **A class event refusing a car that fits, after you switched from a car-pack car.** The server kept the car-pack car's stand-in class after you changed to one of the game's own cars. It now takes the new car's class.
- **A crash in the garage with some car-pack cars.** A car-pack car with a missing drivetrain, body, clutch or tire record crashed the game when the garage rated it or loaded its engine. Such a car now shows as the placeholder car or without a rating (#328).

## Unchanged

- The FH2MP mod is new (shim 1.4.6), and so are the game server and the LSP. The launcher stays at 1.4.3.
