## Fixed

- **Free Roam events online.** With Extra Options 0.6.0, online Free Roam showed no events on the map, and none could be started. They're back, whether Extra Options' Show All Events is on or off.
- **Drivatars driving into walls and trees.** Players' Drivatars used to keep their own pace at every difficulty, so a New Recruit could drive at a pro's pace and crash. Each one now drives at the pace of the difficulty it races at, and faster players are still faster within it. Offline, your game keeps the Drivatars it saved, so go online once to get the fixed ones.
- **Forza Horizon 3 and Forza Motorsport 5 saves.** If FH2 was the last game you played and you then picked FH3 or FM5 in Azimuth's own game list, FH2MP's account went with it, so that game opened a new empty profile (a `User_...` folder in its saves). Now only FH2 gets the FH2MP account. Your FH3 and FM5 progress was never touched; delete the extra `User_...` folder if you have one.
- The launcher installs the new FH2MP mod the next time you press Play. No launcher update is needed.
