> **Very early release candidate.** Please report bugs and feedback in [Issues](https://github.com/Charelene/FH2MP/issues).

FH2MP is an open, community-run replacement for the retired Forza Horizon 2 online services. No lobbies to host, no addresses to share: matchmaking finds players for you.

## What works
- **Car Meets:** meet players in the same car park and start Free Roam together.
- **Free Roam:** match into the first available party and start race events.
- **Road Trip:** vote for destinations and drive to events together, including the "fun" modes (Infection, King; experimental).
- **Paint jobs and tunes:** share them with other players.
- **Car Clubs.**
- **Rivals:** speed-trap leaderboards and monthly challenges. Challenges are randomised: car restrictions, weather, circuit and time of day.

## Requirements
- Your own copy of **Forza Horizon 2** (Xbox One build 1.0.0.64), run through **[Azimuth 0.9.10](https://almost-there-games.itch.io/azimuth)**. Other Azimuth versions are untested.
- Windows, or Linux with Proton/Wine (the launcher finds your Steam prefix on its own).

## Install
Download one file below:

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows | `FH2MP-Launcher-…-windows-x64-setup.exe` (installer) or `…-portable.zip` |
| Linux, Debian/Ubuntu | `fh2mp-launcher_…_amd64.deb` |
| Linux, anything else | `FH2MP-Launcher-…-x86_64.AppImage` (`chmod +x`, run) |

`fh2mp-shim-….zip` is the in-game part; the launcher installs and updates it for you, you don't need to download it.

## First run
1. Pick your Azimuth folder.
2. Choose a gamertag (and a picture if you like).
3. **Save your recovery code.** There is no email and no password: your account is a key on your computer, and the recovery code is the only way to get it back on another PC or after a reinstall.
4. Press **Play**.

Existing Azimuth progress is picked up automatically. The launcher also keeps the shim up to date (every update is signed and verified) and has an **Azimuth settings** button for Azimuth's own options. This is a pre-release: the update check only offers pre-releases to pre-release launchers.

## Known issues
- Starting a **Rivals** event can hang on "Setting Up Rivals Event" for some players. Under investigation.
- No packet-loss recovery yet: fine on a good connection, rough on a bad one.
- Traffic is only partly synced between players. Bucket List cars don't show online yet.
- Not done: friends and presence, Convoys, Storm Island / Porsche DLC online.
- Windows and non-Debian Linux have had very little testing.
- The very first load in a fresh Azimuth folder can be slow while it builds its caches.
- One small server: comfortable for roughly 60–100 players.

## Privacy
The server stores your public key, gamertag, picture and IP address. No email, no real name. Nothing is ever sent to Microsoft or Xbox Live.
