## New

- **Police lightbars online.** With the optional [PoliceLightbars](https://github.com/suderek22/FH2_Lightbars) add-on by suderek22, the amber and blue roof lightbars on its 31 cars show for the other players who have it, and flash by each owner's own Police lights switch. The server serves a copy of its car database with the add-on's parts.
- **Quick chat.** With the optional [QuickChat](https://github.com/Trinculo54/QuickChat) mod by Trinculo54, a phrase picked from FH2 ANNA's menu shows over your car for the other players of your world. Only the phrase's number goes over the network. The FH2MP side is Trinculo54's work too.

## Fixed

- **Stuck on the results screen after an online race (issue 547).** The game server now makes sure race messages arrive: a message the game did not confirm is sent again (reliable delivery, on the development server since early November). The race-over message used to go out only once, so one lost packet could leave a player on the results screen until Alt+F4.
- When a game still sits on the results screen 45 s after a race, it tells the server what state it is in, so any case left can be traced.

## Unchanged

- The minimum versions stay as they are. Encore installs PoliceLightbars and QuickChat from its next release; until then they only work for players who added them by hand.
