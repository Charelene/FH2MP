## Updated

- **FH2 ANNA 1.2.4** replaces 1.2.0; the launcher installs it the next time you press Play or run setup. What it brings:
  - Races driven by Auto Drive finish properly. Some got stuck at the finish line with the total reward showing 0.
  - Auto Drive brakes to a stop at your destination, signals turns at junctions and lane changes, and switches the headlights on in tunnels and at night.
  - No more frame-rate drop with ANNA's headlights on.
  - Set Route To Nearest works alongside FH2 Mod Platform.NET.

- Online, Auto Drive still only drives in Free Roam, not in events or Road Trip.
- Shim 1.4.2 and the servers are the same; only the launcher is new.
