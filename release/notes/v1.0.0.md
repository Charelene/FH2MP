> **FH2MP 1.0.0.** Please report bugs and feedback in [Issues](https://github.com/Charelene/FH2MP/issues).
> **1.0.0 is required.** Older launchers and shims are turned away; the launcher updates itself, or download it again from the release page.

## What's new in 1.0.0

### Added
- FH2MP works alongside other FH2 mods: it now runs on the [FH2 Mod Platform](https://github.com/Trinculo54/fh2-mod-platform) by Trinculo54, and mods built on it load next to it.
- The launcher downloads the FH2 Mod Platform and [FH2 Extra Options](https://github.com/RokkuDayo/ForzaHorizon2_ExtraOptions) from their authors' GitHub releases when your Azimuth folder doesn't have them.
- "Teams: On" in Free Roam events splits players into red and blue.
- Road Trip team races when 4 or more players are in the lobby.
- More rental cars: every Monthly Rivals event offers at least two.
- Experimental "Connect over TCP" setting for networks that block UDP (Settings, off by default).

### Changed
- Online races give 10 times as much race XP as before.
- Everyone races with the server's car database, so a car pack only one player has can't crash the others. Cars the server doesn't know show up as a stand-in.
- Free Roam lobbies fill up before new ones open.
- Road Trip: a new lobby's first drive starts at the festival, and drives start where the last event finished.
- Your existing save is carried over in more cases: older Azimuth versions, Azimuth's backup folder, and your other Azimuth folders.
- Rivals: you race the rival you picked, and an empty board races the world record (#12).
- Linux: Azimuth starts with `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm,d3dcompiler_47=n,b"`. Starting it from Steam? Put `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm,d3dcompiler_47=n,b" %command%` in its launch options.

### Fixed
- Starting a Rival from inside an online lobby loaded forever. You now leave the lobby first.
- A 13th player joining crashed the lobby.
- Whole groups kicked from "Setting Up Event".
- Races ending for everyone when a player in the wrong class sat at the finish line.
- Players who left an event staying on the track for everyone else.
- Cars overlapping on the Road Trip arrival screen.
- Rental cars showing "R 999" on the grid.
- Road Trip lobbies breaking when everyone left between events.
- Cars changed after joining a lobby skipping the car checks.
- Offline Drivatars all showing level 4.
- Impossible career levels and speed records on the leaderboards.
