## Fixed

- **The game freezing right after joining an online Free Roam, on some PCs.** Voice chat opened your speakers and your microphone every time you joined, even with voice chat and the microphone switched off in the Guide's settings. It now opens nothing while voice chat is off, and opens the microphone only when voice chat and the microphone are both on. Turning them on in the Guide's settings opens them at that moment. If your game still freezes on joining, send your logs.
