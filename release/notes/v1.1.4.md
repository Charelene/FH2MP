## Added

- Horns in online Free Roam: setup installs HonkHonk (suderek22, GPL-3.0) and other FH2MP players hear your horn. It sits on the right stick, so Extra Options' headlight and roof toggles move to the left stick once.
- Free Roam players see "Event in Progress" with the leader's progress while an event runs, and "Race complete" at the end.
- Traffic is shared between players online; each client no longer simulates its own.

## Changed

- Players already in a Free Roam session appear as you join, without a "has joined" message per player; cars appear when syncing starts.
- "Event complete" appears after the finishers have left their results screens instead of a few seconds after the first finish.
- Searching again from inside a session puts you in another lobby instead of the one you are leaving.
- A car of the wrong class takes only that player out of an event; nobody is removed from the session.
- Wait at most 1 second for the connection quality check before an online search, instead of 10 to 30.
- The launcher no longer restarts the game by itself. If online features do not start, the shim retries the stuck step itself and the launcher shows one message.

## Fixed

- The Players count reaching 13 and clients crashing when the 12th player joined after another player had reconnected.
- Traffic standing still on one player's screen and then jumping to another position.
- Club tags missing from the Rivals leaderboards.
- Free Roam events being cancelled when the leader changed to a car of the event's class.
- A second game process staying open after the launcher restarted the game on Linux.

Start the game from the launcher: it installs this shim. Starting `azimuth.exe` directly keeps the old one. Azimuth 1.0.4 is still required; setup keeps your settings, saves and other mods, with backups and rollback. No game files or third-party mods are bundled.
