## New

- **The Guide.** An Xbox 360 style Guide over the game. Hold the controller's Guide button for half a second, or press Home on the keyboard. It has your friends, the players in your world, your party, messages, achievements, your profile and settings. Tap the Guide button again to close it.
- **Game invites.** In the Guide, pick a friend and choose Invite to Game. They get a toast and the invite in Messages, and Accept takes them straight into your game, even from offline Free Roam.
- **Messages.** Send a friend a text message from their profile, and reply to the ones you get. Game invites land in Messages too.
- **Private Free Roam is really private.** Nobody gets in without an invite, it never shows up in the lobby lists, and any number of private sessions can run at the same time. The player who started it is the leader: the game shows "You are the Leader" and the crown, only the leader starts events, and the leader can pick Make Session Leader or Kick on another player. The Guide's Party shows who is in it.
- **Voice chat.** The game's own voice chat works again. Everyone in your Free Roam world with a microphone can talk, the game shows who is talking, and you can mute a player from their card in the game. It sounds like a 360 headset on purpose: it uses very little bandwidth. In the Guide, Settings > Voice turns your microphone or all voice chat off and sets how loud the others are.
- **Achievements.** The Guide lists the game's 88 achievements, shows a toast with the Xbox sound when you unlock one, and marks rare ones (fewer than 1 in 10 players have them). Compare yours with a friend's from their profile. The launcher installs the Achievements mod (by Trinculo54) for you; you can turn that off in Settings.
- **Themes.** Settings > Theme in the Guide has eight colours, six of them also as a Plastic version. Other players see yours on your gamercard.
- **Edit your profile** from the Guide: what you write there shows on your gamercard.
- **The launcher works with a controller.** Move around with the D-pad or left stick, A to choose, B to go back, LB and RB for tabs, Y for friends and Start to play.
- **NitroSystem works online, in Free Roam.** With NitroSystem 2.0.0 or newer, NOS works in Free Roam, solo and in online lobbies, and switches itself off in events, Rivals and Road Trip. Other players see your exhaust flames. Older NitroSystem versions stay off online. NitroSystem's speedometer stays the game's own yellow unless you pick another theme on the Mods page.
- **Other players' underglow.** With Underglow installed, other players see your underglow, in your colours and effects. They don't need Underglow themselves.
- **The launcher can install NitroSystem 2.0.0 and Underglow 1.1.0** (both by qiangqiang101). They are optional: tick them in Settings and they are installed the next time you press Play. A NitroSystem older than 2.0.0 is replaced, because it can't run online; your old file is kept next to it.
- **The NOS Kit and Underglow Kit appear in the online garage.** The server's car database now includes both kits. Neither changes your car's performance or class.

## Changed

- **FH2 ANNA 1.2.0** (by qiangqiang101). While Auto Drive is on, ANNA stays lit with an "Auto Drive On" tag, and LB switches to a cinematic camera and back; the camera no longer starts by itself. You can pick another button in Pause > MODS > Installed Mods > FH2 ANNA. If you use Nitro System, its NOS is off while Auto Drive is driving. The launcher installs it the next time you press Play.
- **Races no longer wait about 26 seconds in the car select** when everybody has already picked a car. They go on as soon as everyone has picked. A player who is still picking holds the loading for up to 30 seconds more, instead of using up the time the others have to load their cars.
- This update is required: launchers older than 1.4.0 are asked to update before the next Play.

## Fixed

- **A whole Free Roam world crashing at once.** When you drive a car from a car pack, the other players' games don't have that car, so they show a stand-in instead. Usually that is the game's own placeholder car. The server gave that placeholder your car pack car's tune: its parts, gearing and setup. On November 11 one such tune broke the placeholder's physics in every other game in that world. When a race started, each game set up an AI driver for that car, couldn't work out its braking distance, and crashed: 8 of the 10 players went down at once, and one more who joined afterwards. The placeholder now always gets its own stock setup, so a car pack's tune never reaches anyone else. This is a server fix, so it protects everyone, whatever launcher they have.
- **A crash when another player switched to a car-pack car.** At that moment your game swaps that player's car for the placeholder. To do that it asks the car's model loader for the new model without checking that the loader is there. If the swap came while that car's model was still loading, the game crashed on the spot. It happened about once in 200 such swaps. Now the FH2MP mod skips the swap in exactly that case. The car keeps the model it already had until its next change, and every other swap happens as before.
- **The game crashing every time it loads your save.** If a car in your garage has no engine data, usually because its car pack was removed after you bought it, the game crashed a few seconds after every start. That car now loads without its performance numbers, and the game starts. Put the car pack back to get the car's numbers back.
- **A crash on a car card with no car.** A list of cars, such as the featured liveries, could show a card with no car, and the game crashed while it filled in that card's drivetrain. The card now stays empty. The server also no longer sends featured liveries without a car.
- **A crash about a minute after joining Free Roam.** The game could look up something on an object that was already gone and crash. It now treats the missing object as having nothing to show.
- **Ghosts.** Since November 6, no Rivals or Festival ghost was saved, so nobody could race against them. Ghosts are saved again. Runs from that time have no ghost; drive them again to get one.
- **Stuck on the starting grid.** If the start message was lost on its way to you, the race started for everyone else while you stayed on the grid. If you haven't started a few seconds after the others, the server now sends the start again.
- **Invited to an event with a car that doesn't fit.** If you accepted an invite to an event whose class your car is not in, you were left out after a long wait, with a message to pick another car and no way to do it. You now get the event's car select like the leader, and you are only taken out afterwards if your car still doesn't fit.
- **"You were not let into the event" after you raced.** If you joined an event in a car of the wrong class and switched to one that fits, you raced and still got that message. Now it only comes when you really stay out of the event.
- **FH2 ANNA's Tune Car online.** During online Road Trips and events, ANNA's Tune Car opened tuning even though the game locks it, so higher-class tunes could be loaded into a lower-class Road Trip. It now opens only when the game's own Tune Car would.
- **Some converted FH4 liveries showed as plain paint.** These liveries named the person who converted them as their creator, so when that person wore one that someone else had shared, the other players saw the car's plain paint. The server now names the player who shared it.
- **Azimuth stopping at start with "avcodec-62.dll is not the one this Azimuth ships".** That happens when an Azimuth folder mixes files from different Azimuth versions. The launcher now notices it before Play and tells you to run setup, which repairs the folder and keeps your settings, saves and mods.
- **Crash reports name known outside causes.** When RivaTuner (MSI Afterburner's on-screen display) crashes the game, the crash report now says so and how to turn it off for Azimuth. It also tells you when the wheel mod that installs fh2wheels.dll crashed the game, when the graphics driver reset, when the drive with the game was full, and when a car in your garage has no engine data (a car pack removed after you bought one of its cars), which crashes the game every time it loads your save.
- **A nearly full drive.** When the drive with Azimuth or the game has less than 2 GB free, the launcher says so after Play: saves fail on a full drive and the game can crash.
