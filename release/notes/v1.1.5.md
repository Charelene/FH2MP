## Fixed

- The horn, headlights and roof toggle all ending up on the left stick when another mod (for example Horizon Wave Radio) hooks the game's sound loading. The horn now moves to the right stick regardless, and the launcher puts Extra Options' headlight and roof buttons back on the right stick when the horn could not be moved.
- Joining Free Roam waiting about 15 seconds on "Syncing traffic".
- A traffic model report sent before the join being dropped, which left that player without other players' cars of those models.

Start the game from the launcher: it installs this shim. Starting `azimuth.exe` directly keeps the old one. Azimuth 1.0.4 is required; setup keeps your settings, saves and other mods, with backups and rollback. No game files or third-party mods are bundled.
