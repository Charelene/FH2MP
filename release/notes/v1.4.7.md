## Updated

- **NitroSystem 2.0.1** replaces 2.0.0 for players who have it ticked in Settings; the launcher installs it the next time you press Play or run setup. What it brings:
  - The mod's text reads properly in every language (Russian, for one, showed as garbled letters).
  - With the KITT mod installed, blue NOS speed lines no longer show up in cars without NOS.
  - The NOS Kit is no longer for sale on cars that can't use NOS, or when Require NOS Kit is off.
  - The mod's page and the NOS Kit's names follow the game's language at once, not one launch later.
- **Underglow 1.1.1** replaces 1.1.0 for players who have it ticked in Settings. What it brings:
  - The underglow no longer trails behind the car at speed.
  - The mod's description and menus are in the game's language and switch when it does.
  - The default brightness is 0.25 instead of 0.5. A brightness you set yourself stays.

## Unchanged

- Online, NitroSystem's nitro still works only in Free Roam, and the other players still see your nitro and underglow.
- Shim 1.4.6 and the servers are the same; only the launcher is new (1.4.7).
