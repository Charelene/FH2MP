## Fixed

- **A crash at the end of a speed zone with the DriftZones mod installed.** DriftZones and FH2MP both change the code that runs when you finish a speed zone. When FH2MP's change went in first, DriftZones' change cut a piece out of it, and the game crashed as the zone ended. FH2MP now notices and runs that piece itself.
- **NitroSystem's nitro switched off for the whole game when you start it from Azimuth.** A game started from Azimuth itself, or without signing in through the launcher, had NitroSystem's nitro switched off everywhere, solo Free Roam included. It now works in solo Free Roam and stays off in events, Rivals and Road Trip, as it does when you play through the launcher. The launcher's Offline mode was not affected.

## Unchanged

- Only the FH2MP mod (shim 1.4.5) is new; the launcher stays at 1.4.3 and the servers are the same.
