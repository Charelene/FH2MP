## Added

- **Car packs online.** "Keep my car packs" in the launcher no longer keeps you offline. You play online with your own car database:
  - In Free Roam you can drive your custom cars. Players who have the same car pack see your car as it is. Everyone else sees the stock car closest to it: the same kind of car (for example a track toy for a Formula 1 car) with the nearest PI, with that car's own look and sound. Before, everyone saw a VW Bora.
  - Online events and Road Trip take only cars from the game. A custom car is greyed out in the event car select, and the server refuses it if it gets that far.
  - A car from the game with parts from a car pack races with its stock parts in online events, and the car select shows its stock class and PI. Your garage car is not changed.
  - Your times and scores still don't go on the leaderboards while you keep your own car packs.
- **FH2 ANNA** (qiangqiang101) is installed by the launcher, like HonkHonk: the in-car menu with lights, doors, routes and Auto Drive. Tap D-pad down while driving. Extra Options' own autodrive and hazard buttons move off the D-pad, so the two don't fight over it. Online, Auto Drive works in Free Roam, not in events or Road Trip.
- **Doors, hood, trunk, roof and active spoilers are seen by other players.** Open them with ANNA (or anything else that moves them) and the others see it too, including players who join later. Headlights, indicators and hazards already were.
- **For mod authors:** FH2MP publishes an `FH2MP.Online` interface on the FH2 Mod Platform, so a car pack or tuning mod can ask whether a car or a part may race online and leave it out of its own menus.

## Changed

- **Traffic cars and other cars you can't buy** no longer get you kicked and matched only with flagged players. You can drive them in Free Roam; online events and Road Trip refuse them, with a message saying why.
- **NitroSystem is no longer reported** while it is installed. FH2 Mod Platform turns it off online anyway.
- **Drivatars are real players only** now that there are enough of them. The stand-in Drivatars with real racing drivers' names are gone.
- **King and Infection last 5 minutes** when the event leader doesn't pick a time (it was 4 minutes).
- **Road Trip events show their real lap count.** The lap counter no longer reads "n/-" on events that aren't circuits.
- When the server refuses your car for an event, or drops you because your game didn't finish loading, you get a message in the game saying why.

## Fixed

- **Light sync and horns stopped working when another mod hooked the same game functions**, for example FH2 ANNA. FH2MP now works alongside such mods.
- **Private Free Roam and Road Trip** started from the Car Meet failed with "Unable to allocate a game server".
- A crash when another player's car was updated just as that player joined or left (game+0x9224dd, #345).
- A crash at start when FH2MP used the Xbox services DLL before Windows had finished loading it (#305).
- A car pack's car in the server's own car database failed the part check and lost its bumpers, hood and side skirts (#426).
- The launcher could not be online with "Keep my car packs" on: the game showed "Unable to connect to the Xbox Live configuration servers" (#385).

## Behind the scenes

- The Python game server and web services are removed; the Go servers that run since 1.2.0 are the only ones.
- The game server checks every car's speed gains against what its engine can do and logs what doesn't fit (no flags, no kicks): a way to spot pushed speed without reading anyone's mods.
- The game server tracks each connection's state explicitly (half-open, connected, in the world, leaving), so a connection that never finishes its handshake can't act on anything, and the shim reports long car-loading waits to the server.
