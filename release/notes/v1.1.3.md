## Added

- Co-op bucket-list challenges in online Free Roam: one player starts a challenge and waits ("Waiting for player…"), another joins ("Join <name>"), and both start together.
- The launcher restarts a game that never started its online features, which otherwise sits on "Finding session" until it is closed. It restarts at most twice per Play.
- New FH2MP releases are announced on the game's title screen and on the MODS tab through the FH2 Mod Platform's update check.
- Setup downloads Azimuth in one click from FH2MP's server, without an itch.io sign-in.
- Players on an out-of-date shim now see an update notice in the in-game Message Center instead of a bare network error.

## Changed

- Update the supported combination to Azimuth 1.0.4, FH2 Extra Options 0.4.4, FH2 Mod Platform 2.8.0 and FH2MP 1.1.3.
- Download Azimuth, Extra Options and the Mod Platform from FH2MP's server first, falling back to the authors' releases. Every file is still checked against the same pinned hashes.
- Poll an online match ticket every half second instead of every five, and stop waiting for the secure-connection step after 3 seconds instead of 10 to 30.
- Ignore a session file left behind by an earlier Play after 30 minutes.
- Open the launcher inside the screen and scroll its content when the screen is small.

## Fixed

- Online searches sitting on "Finding session" for 10 to 30 seconds before finding a lobby.
- "Matchmaking failed due to losing your connection to Xbox Live" appearing after cancelling a Car Meet.
- A Free Roam event leaving out a player who switched to a car of the event's class after pressing Enter Event.
- The second player's Start in a co-op bucket-list challenge not pairing with the waiting player.
- Owned cars being treated as rentals, changing their class and performance rating and failing event restrictions.
- The launcher window and its dialogs running off the screen on Windows laptops with display scaling.
- The setup wizard opening a second time on top of itself while installing.
- Multiplayer launch arguments following Azimuth 1.0.4's relocated command-line function.

Start the game from the launcher: it installs this shim. Starting `azimuth.exe` directly keeps the old one. Azimuth 1.0.4 is required; setup updates it and keeps your settings, saves and other mods, with backups and rollback. No game files or third-party mods are bundled.
