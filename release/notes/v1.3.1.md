## Fixed

- **Solo events stuck at "Synchronizing Drivatars... 10/11"**, online and offline. 1.3.0 let a world hold more than 12 cars, and that left a free-roam Drivatar holding a car the event needed. That change is undone: worlds hold 12 players again and Drivatars load the way the game loads them.
- **Cars tuned with Extra Options' aero unlock were refused by online events.** `UnlockAeroTuning` gives stock bodywork the race aero's physics, which raises the car's PI even at default tuning. The launcher now turns it off while you play online and puts your setting back for offline play. Such a car races online with its stock aero and its stock PI.
- The message for a car an event refuses named the classes wrongly: S2 showed as "R" and S1 as "S".

## Changed

- **Drivatars are your fellow players' cars.** A Drivatar drives the car its player last raced, with the livery and tune that player shares for it. Your friends come first, every race gets a full grid picked at random from real players, and every difficulty has its full set.

## Added

- **Memory guard (Windows).** The game can't use more than 12 GB of memory on PCs with up to 16 GB of RAM, or 16 GB on bigger ones (a normal session uses about 8 GB). If its memory runs away while your PC is about to run out, the launcher closes the game before the PC freezes, tells you why and offers a crash report. `FH2MP_MEMGUARD=0` in the launcher's extra environment turns it off.
- The logs now show where memory went: fh2mp.log notes sudden jumps in the game's memory, free RAM and GPU memory, and launcher.log notes the size of Azimuth's shader cache at every start.

## Behind the scenes

- The shim refuses oversized web requests and caps how many run at once.
