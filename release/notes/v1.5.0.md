## Encore replaces the FH2MP Launcher

- **Encore is the launcher for FH2MP from now on.** It does everything the FH2MP Launcher did (your account, setup, mods, updates, Play), and the in-game Guide comes with it.
- **You don't need to do anything special.** Start the FH2MP Launcher as usual: it says it is too old, you press Update, and it installs Encore in its place. Encore takes over your account, your Azimuth folder and your game folders, so you go on where you left off. On Linux the AppImage is replaced by Encore's; restart it after the update.
- **Installing fresh?** Download Encore from this release: `EncoreSetup-2.1.0.exe` on Windows, `Encore-2.1.0-x86_64.AppImage` on Linux.
- This update is required: the FH2MP Launcher (1.4.x) can't connect any more and updates itself to Encore, and the FH2MP mod is updated to 1.5.0 the next time you press Play in Encore.

## New

- **Joining an event works like the game intends.** When the leader starts an event, everyone in the world gets the invite, and the leader's "Waiting for Players" counts down the same 10 seconds as your invite. If you accept, you stay on the loading screen until the wait is over, and then everybody goes to the car select together. Before, the first player to accept sent the leader to the car select at once.
- **Modded cars in events, if the leader says so.** The leader's Event Options screen has a new row, Modded cars: Not allowed (the default) or Allowed. With Allowed, car pack cars can race in that event; the class and PI rules still apply. The invite shows a small wrench in front of the event's name when modded cars are allowed. Road Trip still takes only the game's own cars. (By Trinculo54.)
- **The FH2MP logo on the developers' name tags.** Our developers' cars show FH2MP's logo after their gamertag. (By Trinculo54.)
- **The server side of Encore's chat and avatars.** The server keeps the avatar you make in Encore and shows it on your gamercard, and carries Encore's chat, with end-to-end encrypted messages between friends.

## Changed

- **Cars with swapped engines or gearboxes in events.** FH2 Extra Options can put any engine or gearbox in a car, which moves it into another class. In an online event, such a car now races on its own stock engine and gearbox: the car select lists it in the class it really has then and shows the numbers it races with, and after the event you get your car back as you built it. Your garage and your save are never changed.
- **A car that doesn't fit is out right after the car select.** If you pick a car that doesn't fit the event, you get the message 3 seconds after you leave the car select, instead of after everybody has loaded. A player whose car was refused no longer holds up the start for everyone else.
- **Memory runaways say what causes them.** When the game's memory passes 1.25 times your RAM (and 14 GB), FH2MP records for five seconds which of Azimuth's buffers keep their memory, what asks for new memory and which part of the game or its DLLs holds the big blocks, and writes it to fh2mp.log, so the next crash report carries it. It changes nothing in the game.
- **The game servers do less work** for every horn and light update, every car position they pass on (a quarter less) and every log line.
- **Dropped connections say why.** When your game drops its connection to the server, fh2mp.log now says why, and when a message from the server caused it, which one; the game's next request tells the server too, so we can find it without a report from you. It changes nothing in the game.
- **Crash guards show up in fh2mp.log.** Each time one of FH2MP's crash guards steps in, the log says so. Seven of them were left out of the log before. The game also tells our server how often each guard stepped in (only the counts), so we can see which ones still matter.
- **Crash reports say where the game stopped.** The issue a crash report opens now names the spot in the game or a DLL where it crashed, read from the crash dump, so a report needs less back and forth.

## Fixed

- **"Nobody loaded the event in time."** A player who finished a race and left the results screen stayed marked as a racer, so the next event in that world waited for them and ended without starting. Almost every event that ended this way from November 9 to 19 had such a player. The mark now goes when the event ends, and a new event clears it for everyone outside it.
- **Crashes with car pack cars and parts from mods.** The game could crash when it worked out a car's handling from data it does not have: a car pack car, a part from a mod you removed, or a part the server's game data lacks online. It happened in the upgrade shop, the car stats, the Upgrade Wizard and Auto Upgrade, and when a car was rebuilt in the world (#328, #481). Such a car now drives on its own stock parts, or as the placeholder car when the game has none of its data. Until now only loading a car and rating your garage were protected.
- **A crash when the game put away a car's animations.** The game could crash while it took down a car's animation state after that animation data had already gone (#502). It now skips the empty part. (By Trinculo54.)
- **A crash when you set a route on the map.** Setting a destination could crash the game when the route's XP had no rounding step (36 reports in November, #428). The route now keeps its own XP.
- **A crash about a minute after a Car Meet.** The game could crash while it brought the cars back into Free Roam, because a world's car list moved while the game was reading it (#486). A world now has room for its cars from the start.
- **"Unable to connect to the Live configuration servers" now and then.** At start, the FH2MP mod sometimes looked for one of the game's connections a moment too early, gave up, and the game stayed offline. It now waits for it.
- **Horns cutting out with the TCP connection setting.** With the TCP setting on, the server closed the connection that carries horns every ten seconds, so other players' horns kept stopping. It stays open now.
- **King and Infection running on for one player after the end.** A player who missed the end of a King or Infection kept playing it alone. Their game now gets the end again while it still plays.

## Unchanged

- The FH2MP mod is new (shim 1.5.0), and so are the game server and the LSP. The launcher is Encore 2.1.0; the in-game Guide is Encore's and is no longer in the FH2MP mod's package.
